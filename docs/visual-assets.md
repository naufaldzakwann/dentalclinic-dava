## Prompt final

Use case: ads-marketing. Asset type: editorial still-life illustration for a premium Indonesian dental specialist website, portrait 4:5 composition. Create an elegant photorealistic studio still life of a pale lavender translucent ribbed glass tumbler containing a simple unbranded ivory manual toothbrush, beside one stainless steel dental examination mirror lying diagonally on a curved off-white ceramic tray. Gentle sculptural lavender paper backdrop with soft window sunlight and airy shadows, sophisticated restrained wellness editorial art direction, tactile plaster and glossy glass, close-up with generous breathing room. Palette offwhite #fcfbfe, dusty lavender #d7c8ee and violet #7348a8, natural silver. Composition: objects in lower two-thirds, upper third softly lit clean empty area. Camera slight elevated three-quarter angle; realistic delicate bristles and circular mirror reflection. Crisp, warm, calm, clean. No text, no logo, no people, no teeth or anatomy, no clinic interior, no watermarks. This is a conceptual editorial still life, not a depiction of an actual clinic.
